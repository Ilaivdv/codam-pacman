The Goal
Navigate the maze and eat all the dots within time-limits to advance to the next level.
Avoid the ghosts! If they catch you, you lose a life!
Controls
Arrow Keys / WASD: Up, Down, Left, and Right.
Scoring
Pacgum: 10 pts
Super Pacgum: 50 pts
Edible Ghost: 150 pts
Specials
- Eat the large Pacgum in the corners to temporarily turn the ghosts blue.
- While the ghosts are blue, you can eat them for massive bonus points!
- Use the openings on the left and right sides to escape and teleport to the opposite side.
