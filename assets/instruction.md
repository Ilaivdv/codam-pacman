The Goal
Navigate the maze and eat all the dots within time-limits to advance to the next level.
Avoid the ghosts! If they catch you, you lose a life!
Controls
Arrow Keys / WASD: Up, Down, Left, and Right.
Specials
- Eat the large Pacgum in the corners to temporarily make ghosts vulnerable.
- While the ghosts are vulnerable, you can eat them for massive bonus points!
